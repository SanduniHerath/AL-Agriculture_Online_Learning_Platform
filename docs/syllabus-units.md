implemented from 2017 - 19 competencies. The syllabus term plan assigns competencies **1-11 to
Grade 12** and **12-19 to Grade 13**. English short titles are condensed from the official
competency statements; units 12-19 use the section names of the NIE Grade 13 Teachers' Guide.
Sinhala titles were transcribed from the Sinhala syllabus and should be confirmed by the Teacher.

Official competency statements (English syllabus, for reference):

1. Investigates the contribution of the agriculture sector to the development of Sri Lanka considering objectives of agriculture practically.
2. Investigates the importance of climatic factors on crop production.
3. Prepares a plan to obtain high yield through the management of the quality of soil.
4. Plans strategies for the management of nutrients to obtain an optimum yield.
5. Exhibits readiness for establishment of crops in a suitable soil environment.
6. Plans suitable irrigation and drainage methods for successful crop cultivation.
7. Exhibits readiness to obtain a high yield by optimising plant physiological processes.
8. Engages in plant propagation using suitable technologies.
9. Investigates the methodologies of plant breeding for crop improvement and conservation of genetic resources.
10. Plans controlled environmental conditions to obtain successful crop cultivation.
11. Plans soilless cultures for quantitative and qualitative yield.
12. Plans effective pest management practices to ensure successful crop production.
13. Plans quality food consumption patterns for a healthy life.
14. Investigates pre- and post-harvest techniques for a high-quality harvest.
15. Plans methodologies of animal husbandry to ensure high qualitative and quantitative yield.
16. Exhibits readiness to apply principles of economics to improve productivity in agricultural enterprises.
17. Exhibits readiness to engage in sustainable agriculture.
18. Investigates strategies to minimise health problems in agriculture.
19. Exhibits readiness to plan to overcome challenges faced in agriculture.
